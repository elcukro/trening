How to use Bicycle Coach on your phone. The app works offline too.

The app guides you day by dayA check-in in the morning, then the workout on your Bolt, and after the ride – once it’s on Strava – a note from your coach. Not sure about something? Ask your coach: the round button with their face is always under your thumb.

Getting started

Open the app’s address in SafariYou’ll get the address from whoever set up your account. On a phone you’ll see three tabs – Today, Plan, Me – and the Coach button.
Add it to your Home ScreenSafari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”. The app then works like a native one: full screen, offline and with notifications.
Sign in with Google or StravaOne tap: Google with your account’s email address, or Strava if you’ve already connected it to the app (Settings → Integrations). A Google account with a different address will see a “no access” message.
…or with a code from the emailEnter your account’s email address. You’ll get an 8-digit code – type it into the app. There’s no password. If your session expires, the Today screen shows a yellow bar asking you to sign in again.
Check your thresholdsProfile → “Thresholds and power meter”: FTP, threshold heart rate and whether you have a power meter. All workout zones are based on these numbers.
Connect Strava and your BoltSee below. The app works without them, but you’ll have to log rides by hand.

Today – the day card

The card at the top of Today always shows one thing: what to do right now.

Morning: “How are you feeling?”“Tell your coach” opens the conversation with a ready start (“Weight 80.5, resting HR …”) – add the rest and send. “Enter manually” opens a sheet with weight, resting heart rate and three 1–5 ratings. From these numbers the app works out today’s calorie deficit, your weight trend and signs of fatigue.
Before the ride: the workout and its targetsName, duration, W / bpm / rpm targets for the main step, the weather for your ride time with what to wear, and the Bolt status (“On your Bolt” or “Send to Bolt”). “Show steps” opens the ride screen.
After the ride: how it wentOnce the ride is in from Strava: match with the plan, TSS, heart-rate drift, the coach’s note and an RPE row from 1 to 10 – one tap saves it. “Summary” shows everything, including heart-rate zones.
Rest day and skipped workoutOn a rest day the card shows your check-in and nutrition for recovery. A skipped workout has “Undo”; a past day with no ride has “Mark as skipped” so reports stop asking about it.

Below the card: the day timeline (check-in · Bolt · workout · RPE), the week balance (bars: green – done, orange – different from the plan, red outline – skipped, grey – no ride), nutrition, your last ride and tomorrow.

Ride and summary

Ride screenA structure bar in zone colours, the targets, and the steps one below another (warm-up, intervals, recoveries, cool-down) with watts, heart rate and cadence. “Indoor version” switches to a trainer workout (you can undo it). “Send to Bolt” – if the automatic upload hasn’t run yet.
SummaryMatch with the plan (how the ride fits the steps), TSS, heart-rate drift on easy rides, RPE, the full coach’s note with “Reply to coach”, time in heart-rate zones, and the numbers: time, distance, elevation, power, heart rate, cadence. With two rides in a day, switch between them at the top.
FTP testEvery few weeks it replaces the week’s key session: warm-up, 20 min evenly all-out, FTP = 95% of average power. Enter the new values in Profile → “Thresholds and power meter”; zones update automatically.

Match vs. the note. Match compares the ride with the plan’s steps, so a ride twice as long gets a low match even when the coach writes it was “easy, just right”. They’re two different measures.

Plan

WeekTotal hours and seven days with a type icon, duration and result (✓ done, ≈ different from the plan – under 70% match, ✕ skipped, – no ride, + ride outside the plan). The week’s key session has a ★. Swipe sideways or use the arrows to see other weeks. Tap a day to open its card.
Touch and hold a dayMenu: “Move to another day” (rest days in the same month only; days the plan’s rules don’t allow are greyed out with the reason), “Swap with another day” this week, “Indoor version”, “Skip this workout”, “Undo changes to this day”. Every change ends with a message and “Undo”.
After a change, send to BoltWhen the plan differs from what’s on your Bolt, an orange banner with “Send” appears above the list. Changes are saved to the cloud and sync to all your devices.

Coach

The round button with the coach’s face opens the conversation. Your coach sees your plan for two weeks, recent rides with notes, check-ins and what’s known about you. There’s one conversation covering the last 7 days; “Clear” deletes it.

Above the keyboard you’ll find suggestions that depend on the time of day: “Weight …” in the morning, “RPE …” after a ride, “What’s tomorrow?” and “I can’t make it tomorrow” in the evening. You can type or dictate (the microphone on the keyboard).

Your coach can save your check-in and RPE, mark a workout as skipped, move a workout to a rest day (after checking the plan’s rules) and send the plan to your Bolt. Each save shows in the conversation as a green line, a refusal as a red one with the reason. The coach doesn’t change the structure of the plan and doesn’t diagnose – with symptoms, it sends you to a doctor.

Strava

Strava is where your completed rides come from. To connect: full apptrening.felsztukier.pl → More → Settings → Integrations → “Connect with Strava”. From then on, every ride uploaded to Strava shows up in the app within a few minutes – with power, heart rate, cadence and time in zones.

Coach’s note. After every ride the AI coach writes a few sentences: what was planned, how it went, how it compares with earlier rides and what it means for tomorrow. Every number in the note comes from your ride data.

Title and description on Strava. Optionally, the coach gives the ride a title and a short description signed “Notes: Bicycle Coach”. Turn it on in the Strava card in the full appat trening.felsztukier.pl; the first time, reconnect Strava, because the app needs permission to write. Titles you set yourself are kept. “Fetch last 30 days” pulls in older rides.

Wahoo ELEMNT Bolt

Automatic uploadOnce a day the app sends the plan for today and the next 6 days. On the Bolt, workouts are under Planned Workouts by date. You can see the status on the day card and in Plan (“on your Bolt”).
Manually“Send to Bolt” on the day card and the ride screen; in Plan, the banner with “Send” after changes. Your coach can send it too if you ask.
Heart rate in the nameThe Bolt doesn’t support heart-rate targets in plans, so the bpm range is in the interval name; the power target (if you have a power meter) comes as a watt range. Workouts completed on the Bolt come back to the app; if the same ride is on Strava, Strava wins.

Nutrition

The “Nutrition” row on Today shows today’s calorie deficit (or “no deficit”) and how much protein to eat. Rest days have a bigger deficit, hard days a small one or none, test days none. The deficit is based on your check-in weight and target weight, with a weekly safety limit. On the bike: carbs per hour depending on the workout. This is general guidance, not a diet or a medical tool.

Full app on a computer

The same address on a computer opens the full view with a sidebar; on a phone: Profile → “Open full app”.On a computer, trening.felsztukier.pl opens the full view with a sidebar – the same account as in the app. Extra screens: Calendar with drag-and-drop to move workouts, Season (all weeks of the program), Library (workouts, zones, rules), Progress with the power curve, form chart and reports, Gear and servicing, Trip (in programs with a trip), data backup.

The previous phone view (/i) stays as a fallback: Profile → “Back to the previous look”; from there, More → “Try the new look”.

Troubleshooting

Yellow “session expired” barProfile → Sign out, then sign in again. Data on your phone is kept.
A ride doesn’t show up from StravaWait 10 minutes; then at trening.felsztukier.pl: More → Settings → Integrations → “Check webhook” and “Fetch last 30 days”.
A ride doesn’t show up from StravaWait 10 minutes; then in the full app: Integrations → “Check webhook” and “Fetch last 30 days”.
The workout isn’t on the BoltDay card → “Send to Bolt”; on the Bolt, refresh Planned Workouts (it syncs through the ELEMNT app).
No weather on the cardProfile → Weather → set your location. The forecast is for the ride time in your settings.
Zones look wrongCheck FTP and threshold heart rate in Profile → “Thresholds and power meter”; after a test, enter the new values.
No connectionToday, Plan, Me and rides work offline. Changes are sent automatically once you’re back online. The coach needs a connection.
